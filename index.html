第 1 课 Hello!

课文文本

Hi! I am Jenny.

Hello! I am Li Ming.

Hi! I am Danny.

Hello! I'm Danny.

What's your name?

Hello! I'm Li Ming.

What's your name?

Hi! My name is Li Ming.

Hi! My name is Jenny.

课文音频
【本课知识点】
  • my name 我的名字；your name 你的名字
  • What's = What is（缩写）
  • 问：What's your name? 你叫什么名字？
  • 答：My name is ××. 我叫××。
  • Hello! / Hi! 都表示“你好”，Hi 更随意。
  • I'm = I am（缩写），“我是”。
  • 自我介绍：I'm + 名字 / My name is + 名字。

第 2 课 How are you?

课文文本

Hi! How are you?

I'm fine.

I'm fine, thanks.

Goodbye!

Bye. See you later!

课文音频
【本课知识点】
  • how 怎样，如何；How are you? 你好吗？
  • 回答：I'm fine. 或 Fine. 我很好。
  • How are you? = And you? 你呢？（反问）
  • thanks = thank you 谢谢你
  • see 看见，看到；later 一会儿；See you later. 再见，一会儿见。
  • 问候与回答：—How are you? —I'm fine, thanks.
  • Goodbye! 再见（正式）/ Bye! 再见（口语）。

第 3 课 Good morning!

课文文本

Good morning, boys and girls!

Good morning, Miss Zhang!

Let's play.

Hooray!

Good job!

Thank you!

Goodbye!

Bye!

课文音频
【本课知识点】
  • —Good morning! 早上好！ 答：—Good morning!
  • Miss 小姐（未婚）/ Mrs. 夫人、太太（已婚）/ Ms. 女士
  • Let's = Let us（缩写）让我们
  • job 工作；Good job! 做得好！
  • 早晨/上午问候用 Good morning，回答相同。
  • Thank you! = Thanks! 谢谢。
  • 对女老师可称呼：Miss / Mrs. / Ms. + 姓。

第 4 课 Nice to meet you.

课文文本

Hi! I'm Jenny. What's your name?

My name is Guo Yang. Nice to meet you.

Nice to meet you, too.

I'm Guo Yang. Nice to meet you.

I'm Danny. Nice to meet you, too.

课文音频
【本课知识点】
  • Nice to meet you. 见到你很高兴。
  • 回答：Nice to meet you, too. / Glad to meet you, too.
  • too 表示“也”，放在句末。
  • 复习：Hello! / Hi!、Good morning!、Goodbye! / Bye!
  • 自我介绍：I'm… / My name is…
  • 问候：—How are you? —I'm fine.

本单元重点句型汇总

  1. 打招呼：Hello! / Hi! 你好！
  2. 早上好：Good morning! — Good morning!
  3. 问候：How are you? — I'm fine, thanks. / Fine.
  4. 反问对方：How are you? = And you? 你呢？
  5. 问名字：What's your name? — My name is ××. / I'm ××.
  6. 初次见面：Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.
  7. 道别：Goodbye! / Bye! / See you later!
  8. 表扬与感谢：Good job! — Thank you! / Thanks!
